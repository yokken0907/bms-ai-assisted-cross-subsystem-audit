# Third-party notices

This reproducibility repository audits foxBMS 2 but is not an official foxBMS/Fraunhofer repository.

The upstream exact source identity is recorded in `upstream/SOURCE_IDENTITY.json`. foxBMS 2 reports its software as BSD-3-Clause and its hardware/documentation as CC-BY-4.0. The upstream source itself is not tracked here; it is fetched from the exact commit for verification.

The byte-preserved `witnesses/diag_cfg.h` is an upstream-derived support header carrying its own BSD-3-Clause notice. Other project-created witnesses are preserved for audit lineage.

Exact copies of the upstream `BSD-3-Clause.txt` and `CC-BY-4.0.txt` from the frozen source archive are retained under prefixed filenames in `third_party/` solely for license traceability.
